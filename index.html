About VUSI

VUSI stands for Vu Super Intelligent. It is a personal, noncommercial technology project dedicated to learning, building, and documenting practical applications of artificial intelligence.

The goal is to explore how AI agents, automation, cloud technologies, and cybersecurity can work together to solve real engineering challenges.

This website serves as a public development journal and project showcase. It does not currently provide a live AI chatbot service.
